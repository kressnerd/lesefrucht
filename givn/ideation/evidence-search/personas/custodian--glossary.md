# Persona: Custodian of the glossary

## Stable core

- Role: The same person as the reader and the metadata custodian, acting as the
  authority over the product's concept-to-term store.
- Authority boundary: Decides which terms are accepted per concept and
  language; decides nothing in the calibre catalog.
- Evaluation capability: Can judge whether two terms name the same concept and
  whether a translation is accurate enough to expand a question.
- Own vocabulary: concept, term, language, anti-term. Refuses "tag" — that is
  calibre — and "synonym list", because the store is per concept and language.
- Success criterion: A German question reaches the English passage because the
  accepted term is right, and the vocabulary stays small enough to trust.
